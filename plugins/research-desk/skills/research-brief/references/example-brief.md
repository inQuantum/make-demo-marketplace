# Example brief

**What it is** — A hosted Postgres provider that separates storage from compute, so databases
can scale to zero between requests.

**Who it's for** — Teams running many small databases: preview environments, per-tenant setups.

**Recent**
- Mar 2026 — Series C, $90M, led by an infrastructure fund.
- Jan 2026 — Added a managed read-replica tier.
- Nov 2025 — Three-hour regional outage; public post-mortem published.

**Watch out for** — Reviews agree on cold-start latency after scale-to-zero. Opinions on
support quality are split.

**Unknown** — Could not establish current pricing above the team tier; the pricing page sends
enterprise plans to a contact form.
